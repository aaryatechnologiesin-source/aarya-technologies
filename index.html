Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is your typical project timeline?
Our standard timeline for corporate websites is 8-12 weeks. Complex custom software or mobile applications typically require 4-6 months to ensure rigorous testing and security standards are met before launch.
Do you use templates or custom code?
We strictly write custom code. We do not use generic WordPress themes, templates, or page builders. Every project is engineered from scratch to guarantee optimal performance, security, and brand uniqueness.
Who owns the intellectual property (IP)?
Upon full payment, the client retains 100% ownership of the intellectual property, including all source code, design assets, and database architecture.
Do you provide ongoing support after deployment?
Yes. We offer continuous Service Level Agreements (SLAs) ranging from standard monthly maintenance and updates to 24/7 dedicated enterprise engineering support.
